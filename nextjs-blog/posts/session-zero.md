Before the dice ever hit the table, the most important session of your campaign happens: **Session Zero**.

## What Is Session Zero?

It's a meeting — sometimes a full session, sometimes just an hour — where the group discusses expectations, boundaries, and the shape of the game before it begins.

## Topics to Cover

- **Tone** — Is this a grimdark epic or a lighthearted romp?
- **Content boundaries** — What themes are off-limits for your group?
- **Character concepts** — How do the PCs know each other? Why do they adventure together?
- **House rules** — Any homebrew, variant rules, or table conventions?
- **Scheduling** — How often will you meet, and for how long?

## Why It Matters

A good Session Zero prevents misaligned expectations down the road. It also gives everyone a chance to build characters that fit the world and each other, so session one starts with momentum instead of awkward introductions.

Treat it as seriously as any other session — it's the foundation the whole campaign sits on.
