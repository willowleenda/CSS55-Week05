A magic system is one of the most defining features of a fantasy setting. Whether you're writing a novel or designing a campaign, a well-crafted system makes your world feel real.

## Hard vs. Soft Magic

**Hard magic** has clear rules the reader (or player) can understand. Think of it like a science — predictable, with costs and limits.

**Soft magic** is mysterious and unexplained. It creates wonder and awe, but shouldn't be used to solve plot problems.

Most great fantasy settings use a **mix of both** — hard magic for the everyday, soft magic for the cosmic.

## The Cost of Magic

Every good magic system has a cost. Ask yourself:

1. What does casting a spell *take* from the caster?
2. Who gets to use magic, and why?
3. What happens when magic goes wrong?

The answers shape your world's politics, economy, and culture.

## Magic Shapes Society

If teleportation exists, there are no trade caravans. If healing magic is common, there are no plagues. Let your magic ripple outward into every corner of your world.
